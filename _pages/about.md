**I am a Quantitative Research Assistant at [Young Lives](https://www.younglives.org.uk/).** I am based at Oxford's [Department of International Development](https://www.qeh.ox.ac.uk/)

My research focuses the intersections between policy, inequality, development, and the environment across different scales, using a variety of techniques from econometrics, GIS, and remote sensening. 

I hold an MSc in Environmental Change and Management from the University of Oxford. Prior to working at young lives, I've worked in a variety of roles from aiding livelihood surveys in Peru to researching multilateral climate finance. I have a BA in Economics and Geography from McGill University.
